System Guide & Operating Rules

⏰ Clinic Operating Hours

Sunrise Dental Clinic operates Monday through Sunday from 08:00 to 17:00. Appointments cannot be booked outside these clinic hours. Database check constraints automatically reject any booking attempt before 08:00:00 or after 17:00:00.

🛡️ System Validation Rules

  • Patient Name & Address: Mandatory text fields.
  • Contact Number: Must be exactly 10 numeric digits starting with 0 (e.g., 0771234567, 0719876543).
  • Appointment Date: Must be today or a future date. Past dates are automatically blocked by client and server validation.
  • Time Range: Time slots must fall between 08:00 and 17:00.
  • Anti Double-Booking Rule: A dentist cannot be double-booked for the exact same date and time. This is enforced both at the application service level and by database trigger/unique index.
  • 1:1 Billing Rule: Each appointment has at most one corresponding bill. Re-generating a bill for an existing appointment produces an HTTP 409 Conflict.

📖 Modules & Workflow Guide

1. Dashboard

Displays quick links to all clinic modules and loads today's live appointment schedule from the daily reporting view.

2. New Appointment

Registers patient demographics and schedules a consultation or treatment with live fee preview.

3. Search Schedule

Filters appointments by date, doctor, or patient name. Provides 1-click links to transfer appointments into the billing module.

4. Bill & Cashier

Generates total invoices using database triggers, tracks line items, applies discounts, records cash payments, and prints receipts.

5. Practice Reports

Renders tabular reports for daily schedules, individual dentist workloads, and total accumulated revenue.

👥 Staff Roles & Access

ADMIN: Full administrative privileges over clinical operations, staff accounts, system health, and financial reporting.
RECEPTIONIST: Front-desk operations including patient intake, appointment scheduling, patient check-in, and invoice settlements.
